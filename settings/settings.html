Settings

Gemini API Key

Optional but recommended for reliable card code recognition. Free tier is sufficient for personal use. Get a key at Google AI Studio.

Image Quality

Controls the image resolution sent to Gemini Vision. Higher quality improves detection accuracy for small text (set codes, card numbers) but costs more API tokens and is slower. Lower quality is faster and cheaper but may miss small text on cards.

Recommendation: Start with Medium. If set codes or card numbers are detected incorrectly, switch to High. If you only scan cards with large, clear text, Low may suffice.

Scanning

When on, a scan runs the lookup as soon as the detected fields are filled, so no second click is needed. Turn it off to review or correct the fields first.

Scan History

Card lookups are stored locally in your browser only.

TCGdex Cache

Set lists, abbreviations, and set-code mappings are cached to speed up lookups. Clearing them forces a fresh fetch from TCGdex on the next scan (useful for picking up newly released sets).

About

Pokemon TCG Card Scanner v

Scan both Japanese and English Pokemon TCG cards by uploading an image, pasting from clipboard, or snapshotting the current browser tab. Card codes are detected with Google Gemini Vision when an API key is configured, falling back to on-device OCR (Tesseract.js) — no images leave your device without a key.

Card metadata comes from TCGdex. Prices are looked up from multiple sources:

  • CardRush — Japanese market prices (JPY)
  • TCGPlayer — English market prices (USD)
  • PriceCharting — graded card prices (USD)
  • Collectr — collectible market prices (USD)

JPY and USD prices are converted using live exchange rates from ER API. All data is stored locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded to any server.
